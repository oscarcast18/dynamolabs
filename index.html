About us

Dynamo Labs is an independent software studio that builds time-saving add-ins for Autodesk Revit. We automate repetitive BIM documentation tasks — numbering, sheet exports, drawing coordination — so architecture and engineering teams can spend their time on design instead of busywork. Our tools are published on the Autodesk App Store (Design and Make Marketplace) and support Revit 2018 through 2027.

Founder & developer: Oscar Castillo.

Our apps

Grid Auto Number

Automatically renumbers and orders Revit grids, and staggers overlapping grid bubbles so they stay readable. Configurable, multi-language, Revit 2018–2027.

View on Autodesk App Store

DwgMerge

Exports Revit sheets to DWG/PDF and merges them into a single multi-layout file, keeping title blocks and viewports. Revit 2018–2027.
